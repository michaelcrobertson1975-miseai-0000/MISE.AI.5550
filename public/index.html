What we use

Six things, most of which are already sitting in an inbox or a binder.

01 — Sales

Sales

Total sales Covers / guest counts Product mix Business hours

Sales set the night's baseline. Covers give you average guest spend. Product mix tells MiseAI what actually moved through the kitchen — which is what explains changes in ingredient usage.

More chicken sold means more chicken used, and that's expected. The same sales with much more chicken used is worth a look.
02 — Labor

Labor

FOH labor BOH labor Hours & labor cost

Labor is one of the biggest controllable costs in the building. Separating front from back shows where it's moving and how efficiently the night ran.

This is where prime cost and sales per labor hour come from, along with patterns worth knowing before you write next week's schedule.
03 — Purchases

Purchases

Food invoices Receipts Purchasing records

Ingredient prices move. A recipe that was profitable last month can quietly stop being profitable after a few vendor increases nobody flagged.

Actual purchase prices connect to your recipes, food cost, and usage — so a sharp move surfaces early instead of at month-end.
04 — Prep & Yield

Prep & yield

Raw weight Finished / usable weight Portion measurements

Your kitchen has its own real yields. A category average is only a starting point — MiseAI compares what went into prep against what came out usable and learns what normal looks like for you.

If you record a specific trim or loss, MiseAI uses it. But no nightly waste report is ever required.
05 — Inventory

Inventory

Periodic counts

Inventory is the reality check. Purchases say what came in. Sales and recipes say what should have been used. Inventory says what's actually left.

Counts calibrate expected usage against what really happened. You don't need to count everything every night.
06 — Recipes & BOMs

Recipes & BOMs

Recipes Ingredient quantities Portions & yields Bill of materials

Recipes are the blueprint connecting a menu sale to the ingredients behind it. When an item sells, MiseAI works backward through the recipe to what should have been consumed.

The foundation for recipe cost, theoretical usage, and variance. Existing recipes can be uploaded — you don't rebuild the book by hand.
Optional — Context

Events · specials · weather · your own notes

Events / specials Weather Your own notes

Numbers don't always explain themselves. A storm, a local game, a special, or a piece of equipment going down can change a night completely. Context lets MiseAI explain an unusual night instead of treating every change as a problem.
